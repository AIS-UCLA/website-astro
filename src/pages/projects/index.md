## General Meetings

Every month, we hold a general meeting (GM) in Haines Hall A20 where each
project team presents its progress to the rest of the club. GMs are open to
literally everybody: no sign-up, just show up! Announcements are sent on our
<a href="https://discord.gg/5vHgayXspH" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Discord</a>.
