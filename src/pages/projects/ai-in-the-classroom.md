# AI in the Classroom

**Does using AI help students learn, or just help them finish faster?**

AI in the Classroom is a year-long research project studying how AI use affects
undergraduate learning. We'll run a psychology-style study on real course AI
policies, and turn what we find into evidence-based guidance for professors
setting policy and for students deciding how to study.

## Why

- **No shared rules.** Right now, each class sets its own AI policy, from full bans to anything goes.
- **Little hard evidence.** Controlled studies of AI and learning are new, and results disagree.
- **Hard to self-judge.** Students can't easily tell whether AI is helping them learn or just helping them finish.

## How the study works

1. **Levels:** Turn real syllabus AI policies into levels, from no AI to unrestricted.
2. **Task:** Participants complete a learning task under an assigned level.
3. **Measure:** Time spent, task score, and how much they remember later.

The team finalizes the study design together in Week 2.

## Plan

### Fall

- Sort real AI policies into levels
- Design the study and get ethics approval
- Begin the lab study

### Winter

- Partner with professors
- Compare AI policies side by side in real classes

### Spring

- Analyze results and write the paper
- Present our findings and recommendations to UCLA
- Publish recommendations

## Who it's for

- CS majors early in the program
- Psychology, cognitive science, and education majors interested in AI
- Anyone who wants hands-on research experience

**No technical background needed.**
