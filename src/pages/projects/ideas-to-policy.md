# Ideas to Policy

**A writing group for thinking clearly about AI policy, and getting good ideas in front of the people who make it.**

Good policy starts with good ideas, and good ideas start with clear thinking.
Ideas to Policy is a weekly writing group where members learn how to think
about AI policy, write about it, and communicate their ideas well enough to
reach government.

## What we do

- **Think:** Each meeting, we dig into questions that matter in AI policy, and
  into how ideas actually make their way into government.
- **Write:** The last 15–20 minutes of every meeting are reserved for writing
  something of your own. The best way to learn to write is to write.
- **Publish:** By the end of the quarter, the goal is a piece polished enough to
  publish on our blog or on LessWrong.
- **Present:** Once a month, the group shares its progress at our general meeting.

## Who it's for

Everybody. If you have opinions about AI, you have something to write about.

## Commitment

- Quarter-long, wrapping up with a finished piece of writing
- No work outside of meetings; all the writing happens in the room
- One meeting a week, 1–2 hours
