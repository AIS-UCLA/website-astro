# Technical Paper Reading Group

The reading group meets every week from 4-6pm on Sunday. Each week we choose a new paper and read it before the meeting, and then come together to discuss cutting-edge technical AI safety research.

To maintain the quality of the discussion, we prefer that reading group participants have completed an intro fellowship or equivalent, or have sufficient technical expertise to engage meaningfully with the papers. Even if you haven't completed a fellowship and are interested in joining, feel free to apply! If you are interested in joining the reading group, please message Jonathan Zhou or Kushal Baranwal on Discord (@floatingtrees or @kushalb7).

Alternatively, sign up [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScj0iDnK9suL4IJEe54M52Jo1PSfu4UmBtvEz6yh3amJ1TybQ/viewform) by EOD April 12th.
