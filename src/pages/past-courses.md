# Past Courses

Curricula from courses we have run in past quarters.

- [Computer Vision Fellowship](/fellowships/cv): The Computer Vision track of our Intro Fellowship. Alongside the AI safety readings, participants train a neural network completely from scratch and then use PyTorch to create and train one.
- [Diffusion Fellowship](/fellowships/diffusion): The Diffusion track of our Intro Fellowship. Alongside the AI safety readings, participants work from autoencoders and VAEs up to diffusion models, finishing with a full DDPM notebook.
- [Reinforcement Learning Fellowship](/fellowships/rl): The Reinforcement Learning track of our Intro Fellowship. Alongside the AI safety readings, participants implement policy gradients, work through a Connect4 project, and finish with PPO.
- [Transformers Fellowship](/fellowships/transformers): The Transformers track of our Intro Fellowship. Alongside the AI safety readings, participants build up from the bigram model through positional encoding and attention to Transformers and GPT-2 from scratch.
- [CUDA Track](/reading-groups/cuda): An 8-week upskilling track, from an intro to GPUs and writing your first kernel to putting it all together in Flash Attention.
